Accessibility Statement

Last updated: September 11, 2026

We want the Red Beard LLC website to be usable by as many people as possible.

Our approach

We use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA as a design and development reference. This is an ongoing effort, not a claim of certified or complete conformance.

The site uses descriptive headings and links, semantic page structure, keyboard navigation, visible focus indicators, and responsive layouts. A “Skip to content” link appears when you begin navigating with the keyboard. Text can be enlarged using your browser’s controls.

Report an accessibility barrier

If you have trouble accessing information on this site, please email info@red-beard.com. Include the page address, what you were trying to do, and, if helpful, the browser or assistive technology you used. Please tell us your preferred way to receive the information so we can work with you on an accessible alternative.

You may also write to:

Red Beard LLC
1106 W. Park Street Suite 20 # 142
Livingston, MT 59047

Continued improvement

We review accessibility as the site evolves and welcome feedback about barriers we may have missed. Third-party websites linked from this site are maintained by their own operators.
